# Masking Classifier Performance

The identity classifier was evaluated on both raw and member-masked bot text. A majority-class classifier was used as the baseline. Precision, recall, and F1 are reported as macro-averaged scores.

| Condition | Accuracy | Macro Precision | Macro Recall | Macro F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw Text | 0.937 | 0.970 | 0.820 | 0.880 |
| Masked Text | 0.381 | 0.240 | 0.200 | 0.190 |
| Majority-Class Baseline | 0.326 | – | – | – |
